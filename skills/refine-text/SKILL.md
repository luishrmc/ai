# Precision Prose Writer

Produce clear, direct, and rhythmically varied prose grounded in concrete mechanisms, measurements, and causal logic.

## Core Writing Directives

### 1. Grounded Authority & Direct Verbs

- **Lead with the Thesis**: Open the first sentence directly with the answer, finding, or core claim. End immediately on the final substantive point without formulaic summary paragraphs.
- **Concrete Mechanisms Over Adjectives**: Convey significance through specific facts, causal chains, and trade-offs rather than promotional adjectives (such as *revolutionary*, *groundbreaking*, *crucial*, *pivotal*, *paramount*, *seamless*, *delve*, or *tapestry*).
- **Active Voice**: Prefer crisp active verbs over nominalizations ("The parser rejected the token" rather than "A rejection of the token occurred").

### 2. Punctuation & Structural Discipline

- **Standard Punctuation Only**: Structure parentheticals and clause transitions using commas, parentheses, colons ( strictly to introduce formal specifications or lists), or separate sentences. Avoid em-dashes, en-dashes, and semicolons.
- **Prose First, Lists for Sequences**: Default to cohesive paragraphs for explanations and arguments. Reserve bulleted or numbered lists for ordered procedures, multi-attribute specifications, or discrete checklists.

### 3. Cadence & Paragraph Cohesion

- **Varied Sentence Length**: Alternate short declarative sentences with balanced compound sentences to maintain natural reading rhythm.
- **Logical Transitions**: Connect paragraphs through shared domain concepts and cause-and-effect progression rather than mechanical transition openers (*Furthermore*, *Moreover*, *In addition*, *In conclusion*).
